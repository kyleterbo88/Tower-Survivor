Tower Survivor v104 — Ankh of Resurrection

Replaces Epic Mythic Warcraft with Epic Ankh of Resurrection (10,000 gold). One purchase per run. On lethal castle damage, Ankh triggers before emergency Clear, restores castle to 10% maximum HP (capped at maximum HP), and triples HP regeneration for 5 seconds. The regeneration boost includes HP-growth healing. Emergency Clear remains available afterward.

All v92 gameplay and income-dashboard changes otherwise preserved.

Level details: Expected Creeps, Creep Types (armor and full HP), Spawn Interval, and Spawn Distribution.


v104: Stun diminishing returns per enemy. Successive stun applications have 100%, 90%, 80%, ... 10% minimum duration (no stun immunity). Bounce chain falloff and boss stun resistance remain unchanged.


v104: Reroll button shows how many rerolls remain in the current level. Count refreshes with the shop each level.


v104: Owned Towers shows attack speed buff and Tower/Towers counts.


v104: Normal damage color changed to neutral gray #A8ADB5; Chaos damage changed to burnt orange #CC5500. Chaos secondary glow adjusted to matching orange. Rarity colors and game balance unchanged.


v104: Crown of Thorns 450 base and poison stacks on retaliation; Wrath of Roots 150 base; Thorn Ward Uncommon and 40 block; Bulwark 200 base + 40 block + 10% enemy max HP reflection; Iron Bastion heals 25 per retaliation; Mending Thorns heals 25 per retaliation.


v104: Owned Towers panel now uses the same position, width and scrolling height as the Crown Challenges panel, below both top-right icons.


v104: Owned Towers and Crown Challenges popups are shifted up and left, centered in the unobstructed battlefield space beneath Clear and left of both top-right icons. Popup scrolling preserved.


v104: Level Details now displays exact armor matchups for Light, Medium, and Heavy armor, including damage bonuses and penalties by damage type. Normal/unlisted types remain neutral.
